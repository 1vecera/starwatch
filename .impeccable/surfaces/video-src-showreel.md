# Surface brief: Starwatch showreel (video/src/showreel)

Mode: Persuade (a 45 s film for LinkedIn and the README; the visitor decides to look up their city). Audience: Czech professionals and voters, sound off, often on a phone. Constraints: real captures only, disclosed; Czech-context restraint; the existing 128 BPM score (cuts on bars, final hit beat 88); 1920×1080, 60 fps.

Decisions by Daniel (13 Oct 2026): the app's own light world; one real comparison as the spine; a short, modest hackathon chapter.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The film is the app's own page in motion, not a showreel about it. It refuses the dark motion-graphics reel (HUD, glow, kinetic slams, numbered glass cards) and lets one real comparison make the point: the most-watched candidate in Prague is not the one with the most votes.

OWN-WORLD: The app's page: slate canvas #F2F7F7 with its topographic contours (img/bg-topo.svg) and faint grid (img/bg-grid.svg); real captures in one rounded sheet with the app's --elev-3 slate shadow; teal #0E6E6B only for the one thing that matters in a frame; captions in Schibsted Grotesk sentence case on the canvas below the sheet; Bricolage only in the app's own screens and the end-card lockup. No HUD, no glow, no grain, no scramble, no counters.

STORY: The wall of real campaign posts and the tagline; Czechia sized by attention; on the drop, the real flight into Prague and its party islands; SPOLU pro Prahu's island; Tomáš Kaštovský, 25M video views and 85,284 votes; Petr Hlaváček, 999k views and 100,907 votes, the most in Prague; Pulse puts them side by side: separate measures, never one score; official results for every candidate; open to everyone, no sign-in; one modest line about the hackathon; look up your city. The visitor believes the numbers because every frame is the live app.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The real welcome screen fills the sheet: the wall of candidate posts with the Starwatch card; the camera eases toward the card's tagline. Below the sheet, one caption: "Public posts by candidates in Czechia's ten largest cities, before the 2026 vote." No title card, no logo intro.

FORM: Product film built from the product's own page system (1 of 1; the world was pinned by Daniel, so no direction roll ran). Signature: the camera, not graphics, does the storytelling: eased push-ins on real UI until the names and numbers are legible, cut on bars. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
