# Surface brief: Starwatch showreel (video/src/showreel)

Mode: Persuade (a 45 s film for LinkedIn and the README; the visitor decides to look up their city). Audience: Czech professionals and voters, sound off, often on a phone. Constraints: real captures only for app UI, never redrawn; the existing 128 BPM score (drop on beat 24, final hit on beat 88); 1920×1080, 60 fps.

Decisions by Daniel (13 Oct 2026): the app's own light world; one real comparison as the spine; a short hackathon chapter. Then, on v3: "extremely boring". Maximise the pop and the action transitions of the Mluva reel, keep this design, and show far fewer screens in a frame. The film is judged as a motion-design showcase.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A motion-design reel in the app's own light world. Motion graphics carry the film; real app pieces (post tiles, portraits, the map flight, short tool shots) are moved as layers, not shown as framed screenshots. One comparison is the spine: the most-watched candidate in Prague is not the one with the most votes.

OWN-WORLD: Slate canvas #F2F7F7 with the app's topographic contours (img/bg-topo.svg) and grid; ink #0F172A; teal #0E6E6B as the one accent and the only full-bleed colour; white sheets with the app's --elev-3 shadow; Bricolage Grotesque for display and numbers (the app's display face, weight 720, tracking -0.036em), Schibsted Grotesk for labels; the star mark's four blades as the transition shape. No HUD, glow, grain, scrambles or dark surfaces.

MOTION: Cuts and slams on the beat; masked type that rises with motion blur; odometer counters that land on the app's exact numbers; whip pans with directional blur; 3D walls and planes of real captures; a teal blade sweep as the brand wipe; the map flight on the drop with a zoom punch. One idea per beat or two, each legible on a phone.

STORY: The tagline; the wall of the 48 most-viewed posts and 12,595 posts in all; 5,405 candidates in ten cities; on the drop, the flight into Prague; Tomáš Kaštovský, 1st by reach, 25M video views, 85,284 votes; Petr Hlaváček, 1st by votes, 999k video views, 100,907 votes; head to head: most watched is not most voted; four tools in quick cuts; open to everyone, no sign-in; overall champion of the Agents 0.0.7 hackathon among 38 projects; look up your city; the lockup.

FINISH: the end card's resting lockup is the brand file (0 px diff); every number traces to the live app; a finish review runs before handoff.
